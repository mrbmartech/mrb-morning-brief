🌅 Health & fitness

“A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.”Laozi

Break up long sitting with a short walk

Set a reminder to stand up and walk for two to five minutes after every 30 to 60 minutes at your desk. Walk to refill your water bottle, take a call on your feet, or climb a flight of stairs. Research suggests these short breaks can help blood sugar control and ease stiffness, and they count towards the Australian guidelines on moving more and sitting less.
