Get outside light early in the day

Aim for 10 to 15 minutes of outdoor daylight in the first part of your day, such as drinking your coffee on the balcony or walking the last stretch of your commute. Morning light helps keep your body clock steady, which can make it easier to fall asleep at a sensible hour tonight. Salesforce news

Salesforce signed a definitive agreement on 2 November (US time) to acquire Cresta, a voice-first customer experience platform, and plans to fold it into Service Cloud.
