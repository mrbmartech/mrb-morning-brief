☁️ Salesforce news

  1. Salesforce agrees to buy voice AI firm CrestaSalesforce Newsroom

    Salesforce signed a definitive agreement on 2 November (US time) to acquire Cresta, a voice-first customer experience platform, and plans to fold it into Service Cloud.   2. Salesforce closes Listen Labs and Loops dealsSalesforce Newsroom

    Salesforce says it completed its purchase of Listen Labs, an AI customer research and simulation platform, on 2 November. The two deals add customer-insight and agentic analytics tools to Marketing Cloud, Service Cloud and Tableau.

  3. Salesforce buys Rune Technologies for national security pushSalesforce Ben

    Salesforce is acquiring Rune Technologies, which it calls its most significant national security acquisition so far, to bolster its Missionforce government and defence business. Salesforce says that business is growing 80% year on year, and the deal is expected to close in the fourth quarter of fiscal 2027.

  4. What the Braze deal means for Marketing Cloud usersSalesforce Ben

    Salesforce Ben looks at what Salesforce's roughly $5.66 billion Braze acquisition means for Marketing Cloud professionals and partners. Salesforce says Braze will be connected with Data 360, Customer 360 and Agentforce, so the main question is how the products will be combined without adding complexity.

  5. Salesforce adds agent kill switch and cost controlsSalesforce Ben

    Salesforce has launched an agent kill switch and new cost controls for managing AI agents, as reported by Salesforce Ben on 27 October. It matters for admins watching spend and reliability as agent usage grows and consumption-based pricing comes in.
